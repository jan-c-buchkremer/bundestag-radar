# Decisions

One line per decision, newest last. Date, decision, reason.

- 2026-09-21 — The foundation store is read directly with stdlib `sqlite3` in read-only mode, not via `bdf query corpus`. The corpus query lacks `agenda_item_id` and `person.party`, and calling it means running another repo's venv as a subprocess.
- 2026-09-21 — Sitting week = ISO week of `sitting.date`. Verified on all 94 WP21 sittings: no week crosses a Sunday, a "gap > 3 days" rule gives the same partition.
- 2026-09-21 — Speeches under 500 characters are dropped. Below that the units are "Bitte, gern.", single questions and one-sentence replies; the cut removes 9 % of rows WP-wide.
- 2026-09-21 — Split speeches (`ID…-2`, `-3`…) are re-joined per speaker within one `rede` id. Otherwise one speech is up to six points and the answers to Zwischenfragen become separate short units.
- 2026-09-21 — Fraction falls back to `person.party` (CDU/CSU merged), else "ohne Fraktion". `speech.fraction` is NULL for every minister (15 % of speeches); without the fallback the whole government is grey.
- 2026-09-21 — Regierungsbefragung is embedded and clustered like everything else but hidden by default in the page. 23 % of all speeches, two-minute turns on unrelated topics under one agenda item.
- 2026-09-21 — Embedding model `intfloat/multilingual-e5-base`, chunked on paragraph boundaries to ≤ 500 tokens, mean-pooled. Measured 4 k chars/s on an i7-4790K (6 min per week, ~2.5 h for the Wahlperiode); bge-m3 whole-speech was 6× slower (30 min per week) with no demonstrated gain on agenda purity.
- 2026-09-21 — Embeddings cached in a repo-local SQLite file, one pooled vector per (model + chunk size, text hash). Content-addressed, so re-join or id changes need no invalidation; stdlib only; `.npy` per week and Parquet rejected. Chunk vectors are not kept: nothing reads them.
- 2026-09-21 — UMAP (cosine) to 5-D for HDBSCAN (`sklearn.cluster.HDBSCAN`, no extra dependency) and to 2-D for display; c-TF-IDF top terms as labels with a German + parliamentary stoplist. BERTopic rejected as a large dependency for the 15 % of it we would use; KMeans rejected because it forces every point into a topic.
- 2026-09-21 — Agenda items are a sanity check, not a target: on week 2026-W28, 5-NN agenda purity is 0.72 for e5 vs 0.76 for TF-IDF, and what e5 merges is thematically sensible (Regierungserklärung ↔ economy Antrag ↔ Automobil Aktuelle Stunde).
- 2026-09-21 — The map is one self-contained static HTML file per week with Plotly.js from CDN and hand-written filter controls. Weekly regenerated output does not need a running Python server; Streamlit/Dash/datamapplot rejected.
- 2026-09-21 — Fractions use the conventional party colours although red/green fails the CVD check; legend, hover and filters carry the identity as secondary encoding. Clusters use a validated 8-hue palette, extended with lighter steps because labels, not colour, identify a cluster.
- 2026-09-21 — Speech-level provenance is the sitting PDF plus "BT-PlPr. 21/88" and the speech id; page-level deep links would need a page column in the foundation (the XML has `druckseitennummer` markers, the store drops them).
- 2026-09-21 — Weeks with fewer than 16 long speeches (the two ceremonial single sittings) get an SVD layout and no clusters instead of UMAP/HDBSCAN, which fail on 5 points. Every week gets a page.
- 2026-09-21 — c-TF-IDF labels use `max_df=0.5` plus a German function-word and parliamentary stoplist. `max_df` alone left "wollen · doch · dieses" on top of clusters.
- 2026-09-21 — The five most similar speeches per speech are computed at build time and shipped in the page (5 ids × 400 speeches) instead of shipping vectors; the browser never needs the embeddings.
- 2026-09-21 — Light theme only, Inter via Google Fonts, Plotly from CDN. The owner asked for a light, minimal look; a dark variant would double the palette validation for no reader.
- 2026-09-21 — One static page per week plus a static index; the week list is inlined into every page for the week picker (a two-year calendar of ISO weeks, sitting weeks as links). No server, no build manifest to keep in sync.
- 2026-09-21 — HDBSCAN `cluster_selection_method="leaf"`. With the default "eom", budget weeks collapse into one cluster of ~470 speeches (2025-W28, 2025-W38); "leaf" gives 14–18 clusters there and the identical result on a normal week.
- 2026-09-21 — Filters follow the owner's master-thesis tool (FTN-System): collapsible groups of checkbox lists sorted by count, empty = no filter, OR within a group and AND across groups, ↻ re-sorts by frequency in the current view, search terms become saved filters. Agenda items get their own colour mode; filtered-out points can be dimmed to 10 % instead of hidden.
- 2026-09-22 — Guided tours are declarative steps `{target, text, prepare, done}` inside the page; the user performs each action and the engine advances when `done()` reads it off the page state (`tourCheck()` after every draw and panel change). No event wiring per step, no library. The dim backdrop is a fixed element, not a box-shadow: the sidebar's overflow clips shadows.
- 2026-09-22 — Days and agenda items share one sidebar group („Tagesordnung“: day rows with their items nested); a ticked day and a ticked item of another day are OR-ed like everything else inside a group. Colour „Tag“ gives each sitting day its own sequential scale (ColorBrewer Blues, Oranges, Greens, Purples), light = early, dark = late; the legend is a gradient strip on the map instead of Plotly's.
- 2026-09-22 — Topic colours are evenly spaced HSV hues (s .75, v .92, as in the owner's thesis tool), assigned by each cluster's angle around the map centre so the map reads like a colour wheel. Labels carry the topic colour only in „Thema“ mode; in other colour modes their dot is neutral so it does not compete with the point colours.
- 2026-09-23 — Container image with CPU-only torch (~2.2 GB unpacked, torch alone 770 MB); the model is not baked in but cached on a volume. Baking it in would add 1.1 GB to every image push for a file that never changes.
- 2026-09-23 — In the container the foundation directory is mounted writable although the store is only read. It is in WAL mode, and SQLite must create the `-shm` file even for a `mode=ro` connection; a read-only mount fails with "unable to open database file".
- 2026-09-25 — Lasso selection removed. Clicking a label or a filter name gives the same breakdown for every group a reader asks about.
- 2026-09-25 — Dimming filtered speeches and sizing by speech length are the defaults; toggles live in `state` and only non-default values go into the URL. Reset used to miss the size, label and lasso toggles, the ↻ list order and the zoom; it now restores all of them (zoom via a new Plotly `uirevision`).
- 2026-09-25 — Zwischenfragen are flagged from the foundation's split ids, not from text: within one `rede` id, a part by another person than the first speaker is an interruption, placed between the main speaker's parts. The chair's words in the six paragraphs before decide Zwischenfrage vs. Kurzintervention (WP 21: 509 announced as Frage, 3 as Kurzintervention, 104 unannounced, labelled Zwischenfrage).
- 2026-09-25 — „Nächste Rede“ is the first speech that starts after this one's last part, so a main speech skips its own Zwischenfragen.
- 2026-09-25 — Filter rows: the checkbox filters, the name opens the value's card. Cards show every distribution (fraction, day, topic, agenda item, speaker) as bars scaled to the largest, twelve rows then a fold-out.
- 2026-09-25 — Agenda items take their sitting day's hue family (the hues of the day scales), shifted slightly in hue and from light to deep in speaking order, so „TOP“ reads as a finer version of „Tag“. The 24-colour cycling palette repeated colours in long weeks.
- 2026-09-26 — Phones (≤ 900 px, or a coarse pointer ≤ 1100 px) get the map on top and a bottom sheet (tabs Filter | Details, heights peek / half / full, dragged at the handle) instead of three columns; landscape phones get the sheet as a right-hand column. Same DOM: the sheet wrapper is `display: contents` on desktop. Opening a speech and pulling the sheet to full push history entries, so the back button undoes them.
- 2026-09-26 — Touch on the map is handled by the page, not Plotly: its cartesian plots have no pinch zoom. Touches are caught in the capture phase (the mouse still goes to Plotly); one finger pans, two pinch, a double tap on empty map resets. A tap previews the nearest speech within 26 px (ring plus a strip on the sheet), a second tap opens it, which replaces hover-then-click. The zoomed range is kept in `mapRange` and passed to every redraw, because `uirevision` ignores programmatic relayouts and also let a wheel zoom drift.
- 2026-09-28 — Zwischenfragen (another person's turn inside a rede, not announced as a Kurzintervention) are still embedded, clustered and positioned with their week, but hidden on the map by default, exactly like the Regierungsbefragung (toggle „Zwischenfragen zeigen“, an explicit speaker filter overrides, opening one switches the toggle on). They are questions to one speech; as points they scatter single sentences of context-free argument over the map. Keeping them in the clustering leaves the week maps of existing links unchanged and lets the toggle show them in place. They stay readable from the interrupted speech and the speaker profile. Kurzinterventionen remain normal points: they are standalone statements on the debate.
- 2026-09-28 — The hash key for opening a speech stays `rede=`; `open=` is accepted as an alias because the cards site links with it. Ids that are not points resolve to the speech they belong to (continuation part → its speech, short Zwischenfrage → the speech it interrupted), since the cards link raw foundation speech ids. `#cluster=<id>` alone now also opens that topic's card.
- 2026-09-28 — Links to the cards site are built in the page from ids already in the payload (person id, agenda item id `wp/n/position` → `sitzungen/wp-n.html#top-position`); vote pages come from `decision` rows (roll-call ones use the `roll_call_vote` id, as in the URL contract) plus roll-call votes linked by `roll_call_vote.agenda_item_id` without a decision row, whose result is then taken as yes > no. All new foundation tables are optional, checked with `PRAGMA table_info`, so the landscape builds against an old store.
- 2026-09-28 — Portraits are hot-linked from the cards site (`fotos/<person_id>.jpg`), not copied: with `person_photo` in the store only people with a photo get an `<img>`, without it every portrait is tried; a failed load removes the image. The credit goes into the image's tooltip.
- 2026-09-28 — The entry page gets a period overview: one clustering over all 8.7 k speeches of WP 21 without Regierungsbefragung and Zwischenfragen (vectors from the embedding cache), UMAP 5-d (`n_neighbors` 15, `min_dist` 0) + HDBSCAN leaf with `min_cluster_size` 60 → 32 themes. Tried 25/40/60/80/120 with eom and leaf: 40 gives ~50 themes, 80 ~25 with broader mixed ones, eom at 120 collapses into one theme of 6.5 k. HDBSCAN leaves 38 % as noise; those speeches take the theme of the majority of their 15 nearest clustered neighbours when at least half agree (21 speeches stay without a theme). A streamgraph with only the clustered 62 % would understate every theme unevenly across weeks.
- 2026-09-28 — The overview's theme × week cell points to the week map through the week-level cluster holding most of that cell's speeches (`<week>.html#cluster=<id>`), computed from the week payloads at index-render time, not stored with the period model. The period and week clusterings are independent; joining them by speech id needs no shared model, and rebuilding a week never leaves stale links in the cached period model.
- 2026-09-28 — The streamgraph is coloured by policy area, not per theme: each theme is put in one of eight areas (Wirtschaft & Finanzen, Arbeit & Soziales, Gesundheit & Pflege, Digitales/Verkehr/Forschung, Demokratie & Gesellschaft, Klima/Energie/Landwirtschaft, Innere Sicherheit & Recht, Außen & Verteidigung; else „Weitere“) by rank-weighted stem matches on its top terms (`period.area`). Each area gets one hue of the skill's validated 8-hue categorical palette, in the palette's slot order, and the areas are stacked in that order, so neighbouring strata get the adjacent pairs the palette was validated for. Themes within an area are alternating lighter and darker steps of that hue. There is a legend of the areas with counts (click to focus one). 32 distinct hues cannot be told apart, and cycling 10–12 hues would give unrelated themes the same colour; areas make colour carry meaning. Hovered, selected or area-focused bands keep their colour and the rest fade towards white. Theme identity still comes from direct labels, the tooltip and the ranked list, whose sparklines use the band colours. Inside an area, bands are ordered by the week of their peak. The page does its own hit-testing on the plot (Plotly has no per-point hover on filled areas), so a tap on a phone selects like a click. Month ticks stay horizontal, are thinned to ≥ 58 px apart, carry the year on the first tick and on each year change, and the plot keeps 30 px side margins so no label is clipped. Dark mode does not apply: the site is light-only (2026-09-21).
- 2026-09-28 — Stoplist extended by „habe gegen genau endlich politik bundestag“: over the whole period they topped the two broadest themes.
